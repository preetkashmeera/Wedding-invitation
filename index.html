A letter, not a card

Dear family and friends,

We are so happy to share that we are getting married, and we would love to have you with us as we begin this beautiful new chapter of our lives.

Our journey together has been filled with little moments, endless conversations, laughter, and countless memories that have brought us here. Now, we are excited to begin a new chapter together: as husband and wife.

Our wedding is on Sunday, 25 October 2026 at Sri Ayyappa Swamy Temple in Barkheda, Bhopal. A few days later, we will celebrate again in Shivamogga, Karnataka, with our reception on Wednesday, 28 October 2026, from at Sri Gayathri Maangalya Mandira.

Since the two celebrations are in different cities and quite a long journey apart, please do not feel that you have to attend both. Come to whichever celebration is easier for you or join us for both if you can!

More than anything, we would simply love to celebrate this new beginning surrounded by the people who have been a part of our lives.

With love,
Kashmeera & Preetish

A few practical things

Things to Consider

What to wear

Festive Indian wear : silks, sherwanis, or whatever makes you feel like celebrating. It is a morning wedding and reception will be in the afternoon, so daytime colours are perfect. No strict dress code.

October weather at Bhopal

Late October in Bhopal can get quite cold. Please carry a warm shawl, stole, or light jacket. Shivamogga is milder, but a layer still helps.

At Shivamogga

Shivamogga is milder, but a layer still helps. Little ones are very welcome at both events.
